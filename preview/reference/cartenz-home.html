About Us

We Are Cartenz Cocoa

Cartenz Cocoa is an Indonesian chocolate brand, under PT. Persatuone Komoditas Indonesia. We are inspired by Cartenz Summit, as the top summit in Indonesia. It’s along with our company’s philosophy and mission: To Achieve your Cartenz! We want to help:
  1. Our cocoa farmers achieve their Cartenz through sustainability, fair price, and welfare.
  2. Our beloved B2B customers to achieve their Cartenz through build a profitable business with Cartenz Cocoa.
  3. Our beloved end customers to achieve their Cartenz through happiness, mood increase, and health by consuming our products.
  4. Our team to achieve their Cartenz through positive work environment.
According to World Cocoa Foundation, the demand for cocoa products increase 3% every year. Therefore, We collaborate with Indonesian local farmers to provide high quality chocolate for global needs. Established since 2000 as a family owned cocoa manufacturer with advanced technology and cocoa experts, we process the best cocoa products and become one of the most trustworthy cocoa powder exporter from Indonesia
