# Briquette export company — redesign prompts

Mode: built-in imagegen. Deliverables: two full-page desktop wireframe JPEGs for visual review before implementation.

Company identity, contact details and product specifications remain placeholders. Product visuals and shapes are illustrative.

## A — Original navy / blue / gold palette

Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: full-page desktop website COLORED WIREFRAME / refined mid-fidelity company profile preview, new redesign from scratch.
Primary request: a credible export STARTUP whose core export product is CHARCOAL BRIQUETTES. Design an elegant minimalist B2B corporate website with a SUBTLE SWISS EDITORIAL influence: rigorous aligned grid, beautiful bold Swiss-style grotesk sans-serif typography, restrained section numerals, very fine horizontal rules, generous breathing space, superb typographic hierarchy. Still feels like a modern export business, not a magazine, art portfolio, newspaper, luxury lifestyle campaign or logistics template. Product- and inquiry-focused.
Composition: one tall portrait full-page DESKTOP website screenshot from very top navigation to very bottom footer. Full desktop width, desktop grids, approximately 1440px wide design. Highest practical resolution, tall 1:2 to 1:3 canvas if available, sharp readable labels. No phones, no browser chrome, no surrounding presentation artboard, no multiple alternative screens, no external title strip. Fill entire output with a single coherent webpage. Show every section completely, including all ten quote form fields and the footer. Do not crop the bottom.
Style: flat, crisp, mostly white/off-white, strong but sensible corporate typography (Manrope/Inter or Helvetica/Neue Haas-style sans). Quiet asymmetry confined to a strict 12-column grid. Small uppercase section labels "01 / ABOUT", "02 / SERVICES", "03 / PRODUCTS", "04 / WHY US", "05 / INQUIRY", "06 / CONTACT". Headings are much larger than body, but not enormous magazine mastheads. Restrained radius 0–4px. Flat thin-bordered inputs, hairline dividers, near-zero shadows. No gradients, decorative blobs, glossy cards, gold foil, excessive tracking, huge decorative section numbers or serif headings.
Content language: English. Literal brand placeholder "[Company Name]" with a tiny simple square generic logo block, no invented brand.
Preserve original website features and the full page order:
1. NAVIGATION: clean slim header, brand left, Home / About Us / Services / Products / Contact links on right and prominent "Request a Quote" button. Fine bottom rule. Sticky-nav appearance.
2. HERO: two-column asymmetric layout with text taking roughly 55% and a quietly art-directed briquette visual 45%. Small uppercase kicker "BRIQUETTE EXPORT STARTUP". Main headline exactly "Briquettes for global markets." split tastefully into 2–3 lines. Description "An export startup focused on charcoal briquettes and international business inquiries." Two clear CTAs "Explore Our Services" and "Request a Quote". Hero visual: restrained large reference-style image/render of matte black charcoal briquette cubes on a neutral flat surface, lightly structured rectangle, with tiny caption "Illustrative product visual". Focus is recognizable briquettes with porous dark surfaces; no glowing fire, barbecue food, flames, hookah props, ships dominating the hero or ornate product staging. Include short subtle footer line under hero "Product inquiries · International trade".
3. ABOUT: balanced two-column section. Left small 01 / ABOUT label, heading "Focused on briquettes. Built for trade." Right short neutral company introduction with placeholder company name, and two concise "Vision" and "Mission" blocks. Small secondary visual placeholder "Operations / shipping image" is allowed. No invented company history, production facility ownership or specific operational credentials.
4. SERVICES: heading "Support across your trade journey." FOUR minimal service cards aligned in one row on desktop, fine top rules and tiny line icons, no heavy enclosed card boxes. Exact titles "Export Services", "Import Services", "International Logistics", "Trade Consultation". Each one short service-description line and "Request Inquiry ↗". Keep Import Services as in original brief even though export briquettes are the focus.
5. PRODUCTS: heading "Briquettes, by requirement." THREE equal product cards in one row with neutral rectangular product-image areas: "Cube Briquettes", "Hexagonal Briquettes", "Custom Specifications". Each has short neutral description and "Inquire Now ↗". Cubes and hexagonal pieces visually recognizable; third image can be simple briquette format/packaging placeholder. Required small section note "Illustrative formats — actual products and specifications to be confirmed." No coffee, spices, coconut commodities. Do not claim coconut as a raw material, certified quality, numerical calorific values, ash levels, burning hours, minimum order quantities or prices.
6. WHY CHOOSE US: restrained compact row with four exact advantage names "Reliable Business Network", "Transparent Trade Process", "Professional Assistance", "Global Market Connections". Small simple line icons and very short descriptions; no invented performance statistics or achievements. Prefer thin separators rather than more boxed cards.
7. REQUEST A QUOTE: important large functional-looking inquiry form. Two-column section: narrow editorial introduction on the left "Let's talk briquettes." and short sentence about discussing product and shipment requirements; right a clean roomy form. SHOW ALL TEN field labels, distinct and readable, using exactly these required labels:
"Full Name *", "Company Name *", "Email Address *", "Phone / WhatsApp Number", "Service Type *", "Commodity / Product Description *", "Estimated Quantity", "Origin Country", "Destination Country", "Additional Notes".
Form layout: Full Name / Company Name pair; Email Address / Phone pair; Service Type / Estimated Quantity pair; full-width Commodity / Product Description textarea; Origin / Destination pair; full-width Additional Notes textarea. Service Type dropdown shows Export selected and a hint "(Export / Import)". Commodity placeholder suggests "Briquette format, packaging, specifications…". The two textareas are visibly taller than inputs. Required-field stars on the specified five fields only. Full-width CTA "Send Inquiry via WhatsApp" with chat/phone icon. Short helper "Opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. You press Send."
8. CONTACT: small 06 / CONTACT label and "Start a conversation." Four clear placeholder contact items "[Email address]", "[Business phone]", "[Office address]", "[Business hours]"; social icon placeholders. Map rectangle labeled "Map placeholder". Balanced two-column desktop arrangement.
9. FOOTER: understated dark three/four-column footer with [Company Name] logo, short export-briquette business description, navigation links, four service links, contact placeholders, social icons, bottom copyright "© 2026 [Company Name]". Normal corporate footer height.
Include one circular floating WhatsApp control at bottom right, clear of important content. Keep the inquiry form and direct WhatsApp CTA visually obvious.
Absolutely no ecommerce cart, price tags, checkout, authentication/admin, testimonial section, customer logos, fabricated certifications or years of experience. No fake company achievements, country locations or partnerships. Content is a configurable wireframe. Final design should be substantially more refined, airy and grid-disciplined than a generic rounded-card website. PRIORITIZE all nine sections, all exact service titles, all ten distinct form labels, and full footer. No gibberish text or watermark.
COLOR DIRECTION A — ORIGINAL CORPORATE PALETTE:
Deep navy #10243A, royal blue #245B91, off-white #F7F8FA, light gray #E9EDF2, gold #D5A64A, dark text #17212B.
White/off-white should occupy at least 75% of the page, with navy type and fine gray rules. Main hero button royal blue or navy, gold limited to a tiny label mark, thin accent rule and discreet navigation focus. Use restrained navy treatment on footer and quote CTA. Image backgrounds are cool pale gray, charcoal briquette imagery remains natural black. Small blue line icons. Floating WhatsApp is navy circle with white chat icon to stay on-brand. No green accents, excessive blue-tinted surfaces or gold gradients. Aim quiet, contemporary international B2B credibility.
